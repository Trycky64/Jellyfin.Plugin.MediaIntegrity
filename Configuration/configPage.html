Audio/video repair


                

                    Classifies audio/video timeline anomalies detected during scans and,
                    only when explicitly enabled below, repairs the safest, most-bounded
                    cases automatically. Video is never re-encoded by any strategy. Large
                    or ambiguous anomalies always require manual review.

                        Dry run. Disabled by default.
